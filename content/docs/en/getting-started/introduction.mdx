Marsad is a Saudi platform for assessing business-to-business trustworthiness.
It combines three sources — official records, the trading community's
experience, and platform analysis — into a trust score you read before entering
a deal.

## The problem it addresses

Before dealing with a company you do not know, what you typically have is a name
and a commercial registration number. Both prove existence and say nothing about
behaviour: does it pay on time? has it defaulted before? is the registration
even current?

Those answers are scattered. Some sit in official records; most sit with people
who have dealt with the company and have no way to share it.

## What Marsad does

<Callout type="info" title="Three layers, not one">
The trust score is not built from a single source. See
[Trust score](/docs/en/companies/trust-score) for the full weighting.
</Callout>

**Search and profile.** Search by name, registration number, or unified number,
and reach the company file: official data, status, trust score, and what has
been documented about it.

**Reports.** Anyone who has dealt with a company documents the experience — paid
on time, delayed, defaulted — and the report is reviewed before it counts.

**Documents.** Companies upload their registration, national address, and
certificates, which Marsad's team verifies.

**Monitoring.** Add a company to your watchlist and receive alerts when its
status changes.
